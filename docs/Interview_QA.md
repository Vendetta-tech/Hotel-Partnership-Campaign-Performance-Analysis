# Recruiter Interview Q&A

## Tell me about this project.

I created a simulated loyalty partner campaign portfolio project focused on campaign execution, partner coordination, Daily MIS reporting, KPI tracking, approvals and documentation. I built the workflow from campaign planning through weekly performance review.

## Is this real company data?

No. The operational campaign data is simulated for portfolio practice. I do not present it as internal company performance.

## Why did you choose a hotel partner campaign?

My hotel management background gives me familiarity with guest journeys and the stay category. The scenario also demonstrates how a loyalty programme can extend engagement beyond the core travel transaction.

## What formulas did you use?

I used CTR, conversion rate, cost per acquisition, ROI and achievement formulas, along with SUMIF for partner-level reconciliation and IFERROR to prevent invalid calculation outputs.

## How would you maintain a Daily MIS?

I would confirm the source and reporting cut-off, update daily values, check for missing or unusual changes, refresh KPI formulas, compare actual performance against target and highlight exceptions requiring action.

## What would you do if member acquisition dropped?

I would identify where the drop occurred in the funnel. A drop in impressions may indicate reach or delivery issues. A drop in CTR may indicate communication or creative issues. A drop in conversion may indicate the offer or enrolment journey.

## How would you coordinate with partners?

I would maintain one tracker with owner, due date, status, approval status and next action. I would follow up before deadlines, confirm approvals in writing and keep the latest documentation version available to stakeholders.

## What is ROI?

In this project, ROI is calculated as incremental revenue minus campaign cost, divided by campaign cost. In a real campaign I would first confirm the revenue attribution and cost definitions with finance.

## What is the strongest skill this project demonstrates?

Structured execution. I can organize campaign tasks, maintain accurate MIS reports, track KPIs, coordinate dependencies and explain performance clearly.

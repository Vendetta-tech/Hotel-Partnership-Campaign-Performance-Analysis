# Simulated Loyalty Partner Campaign Case Study

## Business situation

A loyalty team needs to execute partner campaigns while coordinating internal stakeholders, partners, approvals, campaign assets, documentation and daily reporting. This project focuses on structured execution and clear KPI reporting rather than advanced analytics.

## Objective

Build an end-to-end simulated campaign demonstrating campaign execution, partner coordination, Daily MIS reporting, member acquisition monitoring, KPI tracking, weekly performance review, documentation and approval tracking.

## Campaign scenario

The project models an 8-week Travel and Stay Partner Campaign with a member acquisition target of **2,000**.

Simulated final results:

- New members: **2,140**
- Achievement: **107.0%**
- Partner transactions: **1,486**
- Campaign cost: **₹300,000**
- Incremental revenue: **₹356,859**
- Cost per acquisition: **₹140.19**
- ROI: **18.95%**

## Process followed

1. Define campaign objective and KPIs.
2. Create a campaign brief.
3. Build execution and partner trackers.
4. Create simulated daily campaign data.
5. Maintain a Daily MIS.
6. Calculate basic campaign KPIs.
7. Create a Weekly Performance Review with observations and actions.
8. Reconcile partner totals against the MIS.
9. Maintain approval and documentation tracking.
10. Create a management dashboard and presentation.

## KPI logic

| Metric | Formula | Business meaning |
|---|---|---|
| CTR | Clicks / Impressions | Engagement after campaign exposure |
| Conversion Rate | New Members / Clicks | Click-to-member efficiency |
| Cost per Acquisition | Campaign Cost / New Members | Average acquisition cost |
| ROI | (Incremental Revenue - Campaign Cost) / Campaign Cost | Return compared with cost |
| Achievement % | Actual New Members / Target | Progress against target |
| Partner Total | SUMIF by Partner | Reconciliation with Daily MIS |

## Daily MIS workflow

Confirm the reporting cut-off, update values, check missing or unusual numbers, refresh formulas, compare actual performance against target and identify exceptions requiring action.

## Partner coordination workflow

Track campaign status, creative status, approval status, owner, due date and next action in one operating workflow. Confirm approvals in writing and escalate risks through the agreed process.

01 / BUSINESS PROBLEM

Turning campaign activity into a clear operating workflow.

The project focuses on practical responsibilities required to coordinate a loyalty partner campaign. The emphasis is execution, ownership, reporting accuracy and explainable KPIs.

02 / PROJECT PROCESS

An end-to-end workflow built around the role.

01

Define objective & KPIs

Set the campaign objective, acquisition target and reporting measures.

02

Prepare campaign brief

Document stakeholders, partner categories, channels, KPIs and risks.

03

Coordinate execution

Track creative, approvals, dependencies, due dates and ownership.

04

Maintain Daily MIS

Update daily activity and calculate performance using basic Excel formulas.

05

Review performance weekly

Compare actual versus target and assign actions.

06

Reconcile & close

03 / KPI FRAMEWORK

Simple metrics. Clear business meaning.

The calculations intentionally use basic Excel formulas so every metric can be confidently explained during an interview.

CTRClicks ÷ Impressions

Campaign engagement after exposure.

ConversionNew Members ÷ Clicks

Interest converted into acquisition.

CPACost ÷ New Members

Average acquisition cost.

ROI(Revenue − Cost) ÷ Cost

Return compared with spending.

AchievementActual ÷ Target

Progress against the goal.

ReconciliationSUMIF by Partner

Keeps partner totals aligned.
